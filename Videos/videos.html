Push Up

Bodyweight: Upper body pushing exercise targeting chest, shoulders, and triceps

Keep your core tight, lower your chest to the floor, and push back up smoothly.

Pull Up

Bodyweight: Upper body pulling exercise targeting back and biceps

Hang from the bar with shoulders engaged, pull your chin above the bar, then lower slowly.

Tricep Dip

Bodyweight: Upper body pushing exercise targeting triceps and shoulders

Keep your elbows close to your body, lower until arms are 90 degrees, then push back up.

Glute Bridge

Bodyweight: Hip extension exercise targeting glutes and lower back

Lie on your back, bend knees, push hips up while squeezing glutes, lower slowly.

Side Kick

Bodyweight: Leg exercise targeting glutes, hips, and balance

Lift leg to the side slowly, control movement, alternate legs.

Step Up

Bodyweight: Leg exercise using elevated surface to strengthen quads and glutes

Step onto a bench, push through the heel, step back down with control.

Squat

Strength: Lower body compound exercise targeting quads, glutes, and hamstrings

Keep chest up, push hips back, lower until thighs parallel to the floor.

Lunge

Strength: Unilateral leg exercise targeting quads, glutes, and balance

Step forward, lower back knee toward floor, push back to standing keeping torso upright.

Deadlift

Strength: Full body compound lift targeting posterior chain muscles

Hinge at hips, keep back straight, lift bar engaging glutes and hamstrings, lower slowly.

Bench Press

Strength: Upper body pushing exercise targeting chest, shoulders, and triceps

Feet on floor, lower bar to chest, push back up keeping elbows slightly tucked.

Bicep Curl

Strength: Isolation exercise targeting biceps muscles

Keep elbows close to sides, curl weight smoothly, lower slowly without swinging.

Side Lunge

Strength: Lower body unilateral exercise targeting adductors and glutes

Step sideways, bend leading knee while keeping other leg straight, push back to start.

Calf Raise

Strength: Isolation exercise targeting calf muscles

Stand on edge, raise heels fully, lower slowly, keep balance.

Chest Fly

Strength: Isolation exercise targeting chest muscles using dumbbells or cables

Lie on bench, open arms to sides, squeeze chest to bring weights together.

Overhead Press

Strength: Shoulder press exercise targeting deltoids and triceps

Press weights overhead from shoulder height, lower slowly, keep core tight.

Lat Pulldown

Strength: Upper body pulling exercise targeting lats and biceps

Pull bar down to chest, keep shoulders back, release slowly.

Tricep Pushdown

Strength: Isolation exercise using cable machine targeting triceps

Keep elbows close, push cable down fully, release slowly without swinging.

Chest Press

Strength: Machine-based chest exercise targeting pectorals and triceps

Push handles away from chest, control weight back slowly, keep elbows slightly bent.

Preacher Curl

Strength: Isolation exercise using preacher bench targeting biceps

Curl weight up slowly, lower controlled, keep upper arms fixed on bench.

Plank

Core: Static core exercise targeting abdominals, lower back, and shoulders

Keep your body straight, engage core, hold position without letting hips sag or rise.

Crunch

Core: Abdominal curl exercise targeting rectus abdominis

Keep lower back pressed to the floor, lift shoulders slightly off ground, avoid pulling neck.

Russian Twist

Core: Rotational core exercise targeting obliques

Sit slightly leaned back, rotate torso side to side while holding weight.

Leg Raise

Core: Lower abdominal exercise targeting hip flexors and lower abs

Lie flat, lift legs perpendicular to floor, lower slowly without touching ground.

Side Plank

Core: Static lateral core exercise targeting obliques and shoulders

Support body on one forearm, keep hips elevated, hold position without sagging.

Plank Shoulder Tap

Core: Core stability exercise engaging shoulders and obliques

In plank, lift one hand to tap opposite shoulder, alternate without twisting hips.

Side Bend

Core: Oblique-targeting exercise using side bending movement

Hold weight in one hand, bend sideways slowly, return upright, repeat both sides.

Back Extension

Core: Lower back strengthening exercise targeting erector spinae

Lie face down on hyperextension bench, lift torso using lower back, lower slowly.

Mountain Climbers

HIIT: Full body cardio movement combining core and leg activation

Start in plank, drive knees toward chest alternately as fast as possible.

Burpees

HIIT: High-intensity full body exercise combining squat, push-up, and jump

Drop into squat, kick feet back into plank, push-up, jump explosively up.

High Knees

HIIT: Cardio-based exercise raising knees quickly for heart rate and endurance

Run in place, lift knees as high as possible, maintain fast pace.

Jump Squat

HIIT: Explosive leg exercise targeting quads, glutes, and calves

Squat down, jump as high as possible, land softly, repeat.

Medicine Ball Slam

HIIT: Explosive full body exercise improving power and cardio

Lift ball overhead, slam to ground with force, squat to pick up, repeat.

Skater Jump

HIIT: Lateral jump exercise targeting legs, glutes, and balance

Leap sideways from one foot to the other, swing arms for balance, land softly.

Bear Crawl

HIIT: Full body dynamic exercise engaging core and upper body

Crawl on hands and feet, keep hips low, move forward and backward controlled.

World Greatest Stretch

Flexibility: Lower body stretching exercise to improve flexibility and posture

Start in a lunge position, lower left knee, extend right leg, rotate torso to the right, hold, switch sides and repeat.

Hamstring Stretch

Flexibility: Seated stretch targeting hamstrings and lower back

Reach for toes with straight legs, hold without bouncing, keep back straight.

Shoulder Stretch

Flexibility: Stretching exercise to improve shoulder mobility

Pull arm across chest gently, keep shoulders down, hold for 15–30 seconds.

Foam Roller

Flexibility: Self-myofascial release exercise for muscle recovery

Roll over tight muscles slowly, pause on sore spots for tension release.

Standing Forward Bend

Flexibility: Stretching exercise for hamstrings, lower back, and spine

Bend forward from hips, let arms hang, keep knees slightly bent if needed.

Butterfly Pose

Flexibility: Yoga stretch for lower back, hip flexors, and adductors

Sit tall, bring soles of feet together, drop knees out, and hold feet or ankles

Sphinx Stretch

Flexibility: Spinal mobility stretch for flexibility

Round and arch back gently, moving with breath.

Triceps Stretch

Flexibility: Stretching exercise for upper arm and triceps

Lift one arm overhead, bend elbow, gently pull with opposite hand, hold.

Chest Opener

Flexibility: Stretching exercise for chest and shoulder mobility
